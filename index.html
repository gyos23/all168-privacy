All 168 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

All 168 is built to run entirely on your device. This policy is short because there isn't much to say.

What we collect

Nothing, beyond optional crash diagnostics. All 168 does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no analytics SDK, no ad network, and no account system in the app.

If you've opted in to "Share With App Developers" under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple sends us anonymized crash reports so we can fix bugs. This is Apple's built-in diagnostic system, not a third-party SDK — All 168 contains no crash-reporting code of its own. You can turn this off at any time in that same Settings menu, and All 168 works identically either way.

What's stored, and where

Your categories, hour allocations, and calendar edits are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not visible to us
  • Is deleted automatically if you delete the app
  • Is included in your device's iCloud/iTunes backup if you have backups enabled (governed by Apple's backup privacy terms, not ours)

In-app purchases

The one-time Calendar/Donut unlock is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details — Apple handles billing and issues the receipt directly to your device. We only know whether the unlock entitlement is present.

Third parties

None. All 168 does not integrate with any third-party SDK, analytics platform, or advertising network.

Children's privacy

All 168 does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if we add optional analytics in a future version — we'll update this page and the in-app version, and the change will apply only to versions released after the update.
