Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. All 168 has no account system and no server — categories, hours, and calendar edits are saved locally and never leave your phone.

What does the unlock purchase include?
A one-time purchase that permanently unlocks the Calendar and Donut views. Grid view is free forever. It's a single non-consumable purchase — no subscription, ever.

I purchased on another device — how do I restore it?
Open the paywall screen (tap a locked tab) and use Restore Purchases. It's tied to your Apple ID via StoreKit, not an account you create in-app.

How do I reset my week?
Open Adjust Commitments and tap the reset icon next to the title, or apply one of the built-in presets to start fresh.
